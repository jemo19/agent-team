# Backup and Restore Runbook

## Purpose

Use this before data-impacting or hard-to-rollback work.

## Questions

- What data/system is affected?
- What is the backup source?
- When was the last successful backup?
- Has restore been tested?
- Where would restore be performed?
- How long would restore take?
- What data loss window is acceptable?

## Policy

Do not perform destructive/data-impacting changes unless backup/restore confidence is acceptable or the risk is explicitly accepted by the human/customer authority.
